## Why?

I remember when I was probably no more than ten years old, I was visiting "The Book Vault", a bookstore in the 
old Beaverton Mall.  I was an avid doodler back then, and I remember seeing Ed Emberley's _Make a World_ book,
and thinking it was the coolest thing ever.   I never got a copy though, until last week when I finall got it.
But since I'm an aging computer drone, rather than simply setting down to doodle, I thought it might be fun
to let Claude code implement the ideas as a library of code that could be animated.  They lack the coherence and 
odd brilliance of Ed's style, but are still rather fun.  I hope you enjoy this excursion into child-like imagination.

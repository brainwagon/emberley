## Why?

I remember when I was probably no more than ten years old, I was visiting
"The Book Vault", a bookstore in the old Beaverton Mall.  I was an avid
doodler back then, and I remember seeing Ed Emberley's _Make a World_
book, and thinking it was the coolest thing ever.   I never got a copy
though, until last week when I finally spent $5 to order one from Amazon.
But since I'm an aging computer drone, rather than simply sitting down
to doodle, I thought it might be fun to let Claude code implement the
ideas as a library of code that could be animated.  They lack some of
the coherence and odd brilliance of Ed's style, but are still rather fun.
I hope you enjoy this excursion into child-like imagination.


## Get your own copy

Ed Emberley's drawing books are still in print, and they're far
better than anything here.  Start with the one that started it all:

- [**Ed Emberley's Drawing Book: Make a World**](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0316789720)

And a few more of his:

- [Ed Emberley's Drawing Book of Animals](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0316789798)
- [Ed Emberley's Drawing Book of Faces](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0316789704)
- [Ed Emberley's Great Thumbprint Drawing Book](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0316789682)
- [Ed Emberley's Fingerprint Drawing Book](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0316789690)
- [Ed Emberley's Picture Pie](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0316789828), pictures made from circles cut like pies
- [*Drummer Hoff*](https://www.amazon.com/dp/067166249X), by Barbara Emberley, illustrated by Ed, winner of the 1968 Caldecott Medal
